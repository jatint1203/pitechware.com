About Us

Reliable execution for civil, infrastructure and material supply contracts.

Ramavath InfraTech is a professionally managed civil consruction and infrastructure development company delivering high-quality engineering, construction, earthwork, infrastructure, transportation and building material supply solutions for Government, Private and Corporate sector projects.

With a strong commitment to quality, safety, reliability and timely execution, we specialize in handling residential, commercial, industrial, infrastructure, and turnkey civil projects with professional project management and engineering excellence.

Backed by technical expertise and practical field experience, Ramavath InfraTech has established itself as a reliable for civil construction, road works, excavation, land development, railway civil works, demolition, heavy equipment rental and construction support services. We provide complete end-to-end project solutions including planning, execution, earthmoving, transportation, material supply, site development and infrastructure support services tailored to client requirements.
